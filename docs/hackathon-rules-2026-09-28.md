# Hackathon rules and unresolved details (2026-09-28)

This note separates public, currently confirmed information from details that appear to require an enrolled participant dashboard. It is not legal or organizer guidance. No organizer was contacted.

## Confirmed on the public event page

- The Lablab x AMD AI Academy Challenge runs **September 1-December 1, 2026**.
- Participation is **individual**.
- The stated prize pool is **$5,000**.
- The [event page](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-lablab-ai-academy-challenge) links to a live-results dashboard.
- The [live dashboard](https://lablab.ai/ai-hackathons/amd-lablab-ai-academy-challenge/live) separates overall XP standings from Mini-Challenge submissions and grading. It says standings include XP from referrals, Discord, and hackathon participation, while challenge grading is AMD's and is not shown in the XP standings. XP rank/points should therefore not be treated as the Mini-Challenge submission grade or as determining submission evaluation.
- On 2026-09-28 the public live dashboard displayed **“Submissions open.”** The public page does not show the signed-in project form or establish whether SILENTPATH D1 fits an eligible main-track project category.

## Not confirmed from public information available here

The following should remain open until visible in the participant dashboard or stated in current official rules:

- The numeric XP threshold for Legend.
- The exact final challenge, its unlock timing, and its completion requirements.
- Whether one codebase or project can be submitted for multiple themes, and what qualifies as distinct eligible submissions.
- Any rule that a single codebase is eligible (or ineligible) across themes.

Do not use these unresolved details as planning facts. In particular, earlier notes in `docs/STATUS.md` and `docs/hackathon-brief.md` that describe a Legend threshold, final challenge mechanics, or multi-theme eligibility as settled should be read as unverified until confirmed in the dashboard or updated official rules.
